Week Builder

Tell it how you want to spend your time. It builds the week around your fixed commitments.

    1 · How your days start and end

    Everything gets scheduled inside this window. Be honest about it — a plan you can't wake up for is not a plan.

    2 · College & anything else at a fixed time

    Lectures, labs, seminars, a part-time job, a weekly appointment. These never move.

    3 · Eating

    Meals are placed at a fixed time so the rest of the week has to work around them, not the other way round.

    4 · Sport & training

    Say how many sessions a week you want. If a session has to be at a set time (a team practice), add it as a fixed block in step 2 instead.

    5 · Hobbies & downtime

    Things you already do and want to protect: music, reading, gaming, seeing friends, cooking.

    6 · New objectives

    The things that never happen unless they're on a calendar. Study for a certification, learn a language, gym habit, side project. Set a weekly time budget and a session length that feels doable.
